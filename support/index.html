Quick answers

Before you send a message.

Where is my data stored?

By default, your Daytile data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud Sync, your data is synchronized using Apple's iCloud services.

Do I need an account?

No. Daytile does not use user accounts, so you can start tracking your habits without creating an account.

How do I restore Daytile Pro?

If you've previously purchased Daytile Pro with the same Apple ID, use the Restore Purchases option from the Daytile purchase screen.

Can I disable reminders?

Yes. You can manage Daytile's notification permissions at any time from your iPhone Settings.
